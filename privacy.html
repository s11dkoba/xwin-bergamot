How we handle what you send us

In force from 28 August 2026. It applies to the English-language website of XWIN Corporation and to the enquiries sent through it.

1Who we are

This site is published by XWIN Corporation, a company incorporated in Japan on 1 September 2020.

We decide why and how the information described below is used. Under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation we are the controller of it.

2What this policy covers

This policy covers this website only. Our Japanese website and XWIN Capital, our on-chain finance platform, are separate services with their own notices.

3What we collect

What you send us

If you write to us through the enquiry form, we receive the name, company, email address and message you type into it. Nothing on the form is collected unless you submit it, and only the name, email address and message are required.

What arrives on its own

Our host records the ordinary details of each request — IP address, time, page, browser and operating system — in its server logs. We use these to keep the site available and to see abuse, not to build a picture of you.

What we do not do

We set no cookies. We run no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind. We do not profile you, and no decision about you is made automatically.

4Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To read your enquiry and reply to it — because you asked us to get in touch, and answering is a step towards the dealings you are proposing (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), and 6(1)(f) where no contract follows).
  • To keep a record of what was asked and answered — our legitimate interest in an accurate account of our own correspondence (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the site running and to stop abuse — our legitimate interest in the security and availability of our own site (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not use what you send us to market to you, and we will not add you to a mailing list.

5Who else handles it

We use two providers, and both act on our instructions only.

  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) delivers the enquiry form as email. Your message passes through their systems on the way to our inbox.
  • Vercel (Vercel Inc., United States) hosts this site and runs the small piece of code that hands your enquiry to Brevo. The server logs described above sit with them.

We do not sell what you send us, and we do not share it for anyone else’s advertising. We disclose it to no one else unless you ask us to, or the law requires it of us.

6Fonts loaded from Google

The typeface used across this site is served by Google Fonts. Your browser fetches it from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, and that request tells Google your IP address and which browser you are using. We receive nothing from it, and it happens whether or not you interact with the page. If you would rather it did not, a content blocker will stop it; the site remains readable in a substitute typeface.

7Where the information goes

We are in Japan, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data. Brevo is in France, inside the EEA. Vercel and Google are in the United States, and transfers to them rest on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

8How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry and our reply while we are dealing with it, and afterwards for as long as we may reasonably need to refer back to that correspondence. When it stops being of use, we delete it. Server logs are held by our host for a short period on their own schedule.

9Your rights

Whatever your location, you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. If the GDPR applies to you, you may also object to our use of it, ask us to restrict that use, and ask for a copy in a portable form. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time and we will stop unless we have compelling grounds not to.

We answer these requests free of charge. You may also complain to your data protection supervisory authority — in the EU, the one for the country you live or work in; in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office; in Japan, the Personal Information Protection Commission.

10How we protect it

This site is served over HTTPS, so what you type into the form is encrypted in transit. The credential that lets our code send mail is held as an environment variable on the server and is never sent to your browser. Access to the inbox that receives enquiries is limited to the people who need it.

11Storage on your device

One feature on this site — the link that opens our latest market note — remembers a single date in your browser’s local storage, so that it offers itself once a day. That date stays on your device, is not a cookie, is not sent to us, and identifies nothing about you. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

12Children

This site is meant for people acting in a business capacity. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

13How to reach us

For anything in this policy, including a request about your own information, use the enquiry form or write to us at the address below. We normally reply within two business days.

14Changes to this policy

If what we do changes, we will change this page and move the date at the top of it. This is the whole of the policy; there is no other version of it in force for this site.
