About XWIN

XWIN treats AI, data and digital assets as a new form of capital, and redesigns how management, people, information and finance work.
We combine consulting, education, AI and blockchain, and stay through implementation and operation rather than stopping at the strategy.
Working across our group in Japan and Singapore, we build new value for companies and for individuals.

MISSION

Design that supports the decisions of companies and individuals,
and turns them into lasting growth.

When technology moves this fast, what matters is not which tool you pick but what you are designing for, and how.
We take management, people, data and digital assets together, and design the mechanisms that lead to better decisions and growth that lasts.

MESSAGE

From the CEO

XWIN Corporation
President & CEO
Fumihiro Arasawa

Designing the future, from inside Japan.

We hold to a simple idea: design is how the future gets built.

I have worked in education, consulting, finance, international business, and then blockchain and AI. What that taught me is that excellent technology and data, on their own, change neither a company nor a society.

What counts is how they are built into the way a business is run, how people come to use them well, and how that turns into real value.

AI can raise productivity and earnings considerably. But value is created, in the end, by people. Data sharpens decisions, and digital assets and blockchain open new financial options for companies and individuals alike.

We treat these not as technology but as capital — the kind that generates corporate value.

Earnings from AI, value from people, sharper decisions from data, and new corporate value from digital assets. That is what we mean by managing digital capital.

XWIN is not a firm that only makes proposals.
We research the technology, train people, build businesses, develop products, and put them to work in the field at home and abroad — then bring that experience back to our clients.

Take on hard things honestly. Do the work that goes unseen. Earn trust that lasts.
Holding to that, we support better decisions for companies and individuals, and design the mechanisms that carry into the future.

TEAM

Who does the work

Seven people. Every one of us built a career inside Japanese business, and five have been posted overseas. Two sit on the board without executive duties, running businesses of their own.

President & CEO / Co-Founder

Led the global division of a management consulting firm, founded its Vietnamese subsidiary and ran it as president, then opened offices in Thailand, Indonesia and Singapore. Previously posted to the United States. Co-founded XWIN in 2020. Began his career as a schoolteacher.

Posted to Los Angeles / Hanoi / Ho Chi Minh City
Dai Oshima
COO

Twenty-three years with the Canon group. Back in Japan, ran the business for German and Swiss product lines.

Posted to New York
Daisuke Kobayashi
CSO / CFA

Twenty years in trading, four of them as a hedge fund manager. Traded and managed risk in equities, FX, commodities and crypto at Deutsche Bank, the Tokyo Financial Exchange, Monex Group and DeCurret. CFA charterholder.

CTO
