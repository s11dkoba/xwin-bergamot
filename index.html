JAPAN

At the front of Japan’s shift to digital assets

We work from inside Japan’s financial system, its regulation and its corporate culture — from the debate over the rules to putting them to work over a shop counter.

JPY stablecoin, used in a real store

We introduced JPYC Inc., issuer of the yen-pegged stablecoin, to Matsuya Ginza — a department store founded in 1869 — and led the work that let shoppers buy goods there with JPYC in December 2021. It was the first time JPYC was used in a physical store.

Inside the debate over Japan’s rules

We track Japan’s tax and regulatory reform as it happens, including the 20% separate taxation of crypto assets from January 2028. Our research goes to the people writing the rules — among them Tatsunori Ibayashi, a member of the House of Representatives and a director of the LDP Tax Commission.

Inside Japanese companies
